Japanese Sea Scallop Meat

Frozen scallop meat for importers, distributors and seafood processors.

Product Snapshot

Japanese sea scallop meat is available in U15, U16-20, U21-25 and U26-30 size options, with reference packing of 1kg/bag and 10kg/carton. Before quoting, we confirm size, moisture/glaze, net weight, quantity, destination terms, label requirements and documents.

Buyer Confirmation Checklist

Size and Form

Confirm U15, U16-20, U21-25 or U26-30, and whether the buyer requires specific count tolerance, net weight rules or processing form.

Moisture and Glaze

Moisture, glaze, soaking or net weight requirements must be clarified before quotation and order review.

Documents and Market

Health certificate, certificate of origin, plant documents and label requirements are checked by product, partner plant and destination market before contract.

How We Handle Inquiry Review

For new buyers, we first confirm the exact buying requirement rather than sending a generic price. This keeps the discussion useful and reduces mismatch on size, moisture, packing and import documents.

  1. Buyer sends target size, quantity and destination.
  2. We check available size options and packing suitability.
  3. Document and label requirements are reviewed before any quotation or PI.
  4. Only after the requirement is clear do we prepare the matching offer for review.

Questions from seafood buyers

What size options are available for Japanese sea scallop meat?

Current size options include U15, U16-20, U21-25 and U26-30. Final availability is confirmed before quotation and order confirmation.

How is the scallop meat packed?

The packing reference is 1kg per bag and 10kg per carton. Carton marks, label language and other packing requirements are confirmed per buyer and destination market.

What should buyers confirm before asking for a quote?

Please confirm target size, estimated quantity, moisture or glaze requirement, destination terms, required documents, label requirements and expected shipment window. We do not commit price, payment or documents before review.

Looking for another seafood product?

We are a trading and sourcing company. Send a requirement beyond our catalog for supplier search and quotation coordination.
