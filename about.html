Who We Are

Our role is to find suitable supply, coordinate quotations and support agreed transactions for global buyers. You can send seafood requirements beyond our catalog. Learn about our sourcing service.

Yantai Dingdingmiao Seafood (烟台叮叮喵水产) is a seafood trading and sourcing company based in Yantai, Shandong Province — an important seafood processing area in China. We help overseas buyers clarify specifications, compare supply options and prepare export documentation.

We work with qualified partner plants and verify product specifications, plant documents, packing requirements and shipment details before each order is confirmed. Certification availability depends on product, plant and destination market.

We communicate in Chinese and English, helping buyers reduce misunderstanding around product form, size, packing, payment terms, shipping documents and inspection requirements.
