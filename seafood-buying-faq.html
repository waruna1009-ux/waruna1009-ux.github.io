Seafood Buying & Sourcing FAQ

Practical answers before you request a quotation.

Yantai Dingdingmiao Seafood is a seafood trading and sourcing company in Yantai, China. These answers explain our inquiry process and what to agree for each transaction.

Company and service information updated 23 October 2026.

Can you source seafood that is not listed on your website?

Yantai Dingdingmiao Seafood welcomes seafood requests beyond its catalog. We are a trading and sourcing company in Yantai, China. Send the product name or a reference specification, quantity and destination so we can investigate supply options and coordinate quotations. Availability must be confirmed before an order.

What information should I send to request a quotation?

Include your company and contact name, product or species, size and processing form, quantity, packing and net weight, destination country and port, shipment window and required documents. If a specification is undecided, identify what is fixed and what alternatives you can consider.

Can I start with a photo or an incomplete specification?

Yes. A photo, previous specification or label can help start the discussion. We clarify the species, product form, dimensions or grade, packing and intended use before comparing offers. A photo alone does not confirm identity, quality or suitability.

Is there one minimum order quantity for every product?

No single minimum order quantity applies across the catalog. We check the requested product, supplier, packing, available lot and loading plan before confirming a quantity. Tell us your target volume and whether alternatives or mixed products can be considered.

Does a product page mean the seafood is currently in stock?

No. Product pages describe sourcing examples and inquiry options. Stock, origin, processing plant, size, packing, price and shipment timing need confirmation for the specific quotation. Partner suppliers and their inventory are not presented as factories or stock owned by Dingdingmiao.

How should I compare seafood quotations?

Compare the same species, processing form, size or grade, net weight, glaze where relevant, packing, quantity and delivery basis. Check the named port or place, currency, validity, payment terms and included services. A price comparison is incomplete when these fields differ or are missing.

How are a sourcing fee or commission and product price agreed?

The service scope, quotation responsibilities and any fee or commission basis are agreed before cooperation. Product price, payment terms, inspection scope, freight and shipment responsibilities are clarified for the individual transaction. The website does not publish a universal commission rate.

Can I assume that a certificate applies to every product?

No. We request and review the documents relevant to the proposed product, supplier or processing plant and destination. A certificate mentioned in a discussion or shown for one plant must not be treated as approval for every product or market. Ask for the specific documents needed for your order.

Do you accept inquiries from buyers outside China?

Yes. Dingdingmiao welcomes inquiries from importers, wholesalers, distributors and processors worldwide. We review supply origin, processing location, product requirements and destination documents for each request. This is an inquiry service, not a promise that every product is available for every destination.

Can inspection, packing or shipment support be included?

These services can be discussed as part of the agreed transaction. Specify packing, labels, inspection evidence, loading photos, documents and shipment requirements in your inquiry so responsibilities and any additional costs can be clarified before confirmation.
