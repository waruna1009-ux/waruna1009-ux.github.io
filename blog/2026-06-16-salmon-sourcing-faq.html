Frozen Salmon RFQ: Six Specification Decisions to Make First

Published 2026-06-16 · Updated 2026-10-23
Published by Yantai Dingdingmiao Seafood

Before requesting frozen salmon offers, define identity, product cut, piece size, customer-specific quality criteria, packing and destination. Leave undecided fields open for discussion and ask the supplier to identify alternatives. A generic salmon description cannot establish an equivalent quotation.

In this guide

  2. 2. Cut and preparation
  3. 3. Size and measurement
  4. 4. Your customer’s quality criteria

1. Identity and source requirement

Give the species or scientific name where available and state whether a particular origin is required. Treat Atlantic or Pacific wording as the beginning of a specification, not the complete one. Ask which species and source are actually proposed and request the relevant records.

Do not infer that all species within a broad salmon grouping share the same production method, fat level, season or intended use. If alternatives are acceptable, list them separately.

2. Cut and preparation

State whether you want whole fish, headed-and-gutted fish, fillets, portions, bellies, trims or another cut. For a fillet, clarify skin, pin-bone and trim requirements. For offcuts, define the included material and acceptable variation; a low unit price is not useful if the material does not suit your line.

3. Size and measurement

Put the unit next to the piece-weight range. If shape or thickness matters to portioning or packing, include the measurement location and tolerance. For a proposed size alternative, ask how many pieces or packs it changes in the intended carton arrangement.

4. Your customer’s quality criteria

List the criteria your customer actually needs and how they will be evaluated. If fat, color or processing yield matters, specify a reference and an agreed test method. There is no universal fat percentage, color score or thawing-yield threshold for every salmon buyer.

A commercial grade or appealing photograph is not a food-safety assessment. Intended raw consumption requires a separate, product-specific food-safety review; this buying guide does not establish suitability.

5. Freezing and packing

Describe loose frozen portions, an agreed block arrangement, separate bags or vacuum packing as applicable. State inner-pack counts, seafood weight excluding glaze, outer cartons and label requirements. Packaging and freezing format should be described as separate fields instead of using one as a substitute for the other.

6. Destination and order conditions

Supply the country and port or delivery place, quantity and required shipment window. Ask the importer to identify applicable requirements, and ask the supplier which requested documents can be supported. A named certificate does not establish compliance for all products or plants.

Send these six fields to Dingdingmiao with your mandatory conditions and permitted alternatives. We can review the brief, investigate supply and coordinate a quotation discussion; product availability and service scope remain subject to confirmation.

Buyer questions

Should I include a fat or color requirement in every salmon RFQ?

Include it when it matters to your customer or process, with the reference and measurement method. Do not substitute a generic percentage or score for your actual requirement.

Can I compare a belly or trim offer with a portion price?

Only after accounting for the different cut, usable material, specification and application. They should not be treated as identical products based on price alone.

This is a procurement checklist. Confirm the actual product, applicable requirements and transaction terms with the responsible parties before ordering.
