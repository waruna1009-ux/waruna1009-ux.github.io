Frozen Sardine Buying Guide: Species, Count and Quotation Questions

Published 2026-07-13 · Updated 2026-10-23
Published by Yantai Dingdingmiao Seafood

A sardine inquiry should identify the species, preparation and count or size basis, then state seafood weight, packing, quantity and destination. Check that the proposed lot and records match the requirement. Do not turn a generic size table, shelf-life figure or country preference into a purchase standard.

In this guide
  1. Confirm identity and intended use
  2. Define count with the weighing basis
  3. Keep packing, storage instructions and shelf life specific
  4. Review a current quotation

Confirm identity and intended use

Ask for the scientific name and declared source of the proposed fish. Commercial sardine terminology can cover different products in different contexts; the specification should identify what is actually offered. State whether the product is for distribution, repacking or further processing.

Request alternatives separately if your buyer can accept them. An alternative species or form may need its own customer approval and document review before it can be considered equivalent.

Define count with the weighing basis

If the request uses pieces per kilogram, state that explicitly and agree the sampling and weight basis. Within the same counting method, a lower count generally corresponds to larger pieces, but a broad count range does not describe every aspect of the lot.

Specify whole round, gutted or another preparation and ask how this affects the grade. Do not compare a whole-fish count with a prepared-product weight as if they were the same specification.

Keep packing, storage instructions and shelf life specific

Request the seafood weight excluding glaze, inner packing, carton configuration and lot labeling. Ask for the proposed product’s documented storage instructions, production information and shelf-life basis. There is no single shelf-life statement this website can confirm for all sardine products.

The parties responsible for handling and transport should agree the relevant cold-chain requirements and records. A specification review or visual check alone cannot establish food safety.

Do not use a universal tariff code or generic country checklist to decide import eligibility. Have the importer or customs adviser confirm the current classification and requirements for the actual product.

Buyer questions

Is there one fixed MOQ or shelf life for all frozen sardines?

No. Quantity, packing, available lots and documented product storage and shelf-life information must be confirmed for the proposed transaction.

Does a general sardine guide confirm my shipment’s import requirements?

No. Requirements depend on the actual product, route and destination. Ask the importer and relevant advisers to confirm the current conditions.

This is a procurement checklist. Confirm the actual product, applicable requirements and transaction terms with the responsible parties before ordering.
