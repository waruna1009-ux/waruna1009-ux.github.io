Supplier & shipment checks

Salmon Supplier Review: From First Offer to an Order-Ready File

Published 2026-07-06 · Updated 2026-10-23
Published by Yantai Dingdingmiao Seafood

Review a salmon supplier in stages: first the specification and quotation, then sample or product evidence, then the records and responsibilities for the proposed order. Keep a list of unresolved items and close them before commitment. A saved quotation is not proof that the shipment is ready.

In this guide
  1. Stage 1: check whether the offer answers your brief
  2. Stage 2: define what the sample will establish
  3. Stage 3: connect documents to the actual product
  4. Stage 4: close the open-points list

Stage 1: check whether the offer answers your brief

Compare species, form, size, trim, seafood weight, packing, quantity and destination against the same request. Mark each field matched, changed or unconfirmed. Ask for a revised specification if a substitution changes the product you intend to buy.

Commercial review should also identify the seller, currency, price unit, validity, proposed payment terms and included services. Do not treat an indicative discussion as an accepted final order.

Stage 2: define what the sample will establish

Agree the sample reference, lot information, packaging and evaluation criteria. Decide who will record the results and how differences will be handled. If your application requires a processing trial, define the process and measurements before interpreting a yield result.

Ask which aspects of the production lot can differ from the sample. Retain the agreed specification and approval notes together. Photos, appearance or a taste test do not substitute for the product-specific food-safety checks that may apply.

Stage 3: connect documents to the actual product

Record to reviewMatching question
Specification and labelDo identity, cut, weight, packing and lot details agree?
Proposed facility informationIs it the party involved in this product and process?
Requested certificates or reportsWhat is their scope, validity and connection to this order?
Packing and loading planDoes the planned quantity match the agreed configuration?
Inspection recordWho performed the agreed checks, when, on what material and by which method?

Ask the importer or responsible adviser to confirm the current required document set. Do not use a generic certificate bundle as proof that every destination requirement has been met.

Stage 4: close the open-points list

Keep one table with the unresolved item, responsible party, evidence needed and target date. Examples include a missing packing drawing, an unanswered origin question or a label awaiting approval. When a material item is still open, identify that condition explicitly in the quotation discussion.

Buyer questions

Does a sample approval automatically approve the full shipment?

No. Record what the sample established and which characteristics, records and inspections must still be checked for the production lot.

What should I do when a supplier document is incomplete?

Keep the relevant item unconfirmed, identify the missing evidence and ask the responsible party to clarify it before treating the order file as complete.

This is a procurement checklist. Confirm the actual product, applicable requirements and transaction terms with the responsible parties before ordering.
