Frozen Squid Specifications: Tubes, Rings, Size Units and Packing

Published 2026-07-12 · Updated 2026-10-23
Published by Yantai Dingdingmiao Seafood

For frozen squid, identify the species and product form before specifying size. A tube length, ring diameter and whole-squid weight describe different products. Put the measurement, unit and tolerance on the request, and ask suppliers to disclose changes or treatments explicitly.

In this guide
  1. Name the product form precisely
  2. Match the size field to the product
  3. Separate treatments, glaze and seafood weight
  4. Describe packing and review readiness

Name the product form precisely

Describe whole squid, cleaned whole, tubes, tubes with tentacles, rings, strips, wings or another preparation. Explain what is included, such as skin, fins, tentacles or other parts, rather than relying only on an abbreviation. Request confirmation of the scientific name against the proposed product information.

If you accept several species or preparations, ask for separate alternatives. Do not assume they have identical dimensions, handling characteristics or suitability for your application.

Match the size field to the product

ProductUseful size fields to define
TubesLength, wall thickness or count if relevant, with the measurement point and unit.
RingsDiameter, width and thickness, and whether they are cut or otherwise formed.
Whole squidIndividual weight or a clearly defined count and size range.
Strips or other cutsDimensions, tolerance and included parts.

Supplier grades may use different definitions. Ask for the written definition and an example measurement. Do not label one set of numbers as a universal industry standard.

Separate treatments, glaze and seafood weight

Request the declared ingredients and treatment information relevant to your product and customer requirements. An additive-free or treatment-free requirement should be stated clearly and supported by appropriate evidence. A photograph of white flesh cannot confirm a treatment history.

Record the seafood weight excluding glaze, the glaze specification and its calculation basis. If moisture or thawing yield matters, agree its own evaluation method. Those measures are not interchangeable.

Describe packing and review readiness

Specify loose pieces, blocks, bags or other required arrangements, inner-pack count, carton weight and labeling. Ask for a proposed loading plan based on the actual quantity and destination. Neither a generic reefer tonnage nor a transit-time table proves a shipment can be booked as requested.

The importer should confirm applicable requirements for the product and destination. Review the proposed product records and relevant documents before treating an offer as order-ready. Availability and timing require a current inquiry.

Send Dingdingmiao the squid form, measurements, packing, quantity and destination. We can help clarify missing fields and investigate supply options, including forms not displayed in the catalog.

Buyer questions

Is a tube count enough to describe squid size?

Only if the supplier defines its count basis and the definition meets your requirement. Length or thickness may also matter to your customer.

Can photographs prove that squid has no added treatment?

No. Request the declared product specification and relevant evidence. Appearance alone does not establish ingredients, processing history or safety.

This is a procurement checklist. Confirm the actual product, applicable requirements and transaction terms with the responsible parties before ordering.
