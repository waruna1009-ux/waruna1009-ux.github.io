Supplier & shipment checks

Sourcing Salmon through China: Origin, Processing and Order Scope

Published 2026-07-04 · Updated 2026-10-23
Published by Yantai Dingdingmiao Seafood

For a salmon offer handled through China, clarify raw material origin, processing location and the actual product being sold. Identify who performs each service and which records will support the shipment. The trading company’s location alone does not establish the salmon’s origin.

In this guide
  1. Draw the transaction before comparing suppliers
  2. Define the finished product
  3. Separate production planning from a shipping estimate
  4. Make responsibilities visible

Draw the transaction before comparing suppliers

Write down the proposed raw material supplier, processor, seller, exporter and importer. One party may perform several roles, but ask rather than assume. Identify who receives the purchase order and who is responsible for answering specification and document questions.

If processing or repacking is proposed after raw material is obtained, confirm which stages are included in the price and whether records will link those stages to the finished lot.

Define the finished product

Buying needQuestions to settle
Fillets or portionsSkin, pin bones, trim, piece size, tolerance and packing.
Whole or headed-and-gutted materialPreparation, size basis and suitability for your processing equipment.
Bellies, trims or other cutsIncluded material, proportions or ranges and the intended application.
A custom requirementDrawing or reference sample, measurable criteria and approval of proposed alternatives.

These are inquiry categories, not a statement that any particular stock is available. An offer should name the actual product and list exceptions to your requested specification.

Separate production planning from a shipping estimate

Ask which dates depend on raw material, processing capacity, packaging approval or a transport booking. Record a proposed production window separately from departure and arrival estimates. Generic seasonal claims or sailing-time tables cannot confirm these dates.

For sample evaluation, agree which characteristics the sample represents and which must be confirmed for the production lot. A sample is evidence about that sample, not automatic approval of every future shipment.

Make responsibilities visible

List who will check product identity, records, labels, inspection requirements, loading evidence and shipment documents. Ask the importer to confirm current destination conditions. A sourcing service can coordinate requests without owning the processing plant or replacing the importer’s responsibilities.

Buyer questions

Does sourcing through China mean the salmon originated in China?

No. Confirm raw material origin, processing location and export arrangements separately with the applicable records.

Does working with a trader remove the need to review the processor?

No. The proposed processing party and relevant product records still need review. Agree which checks each party will perform.

This is a procurement checklist. Confirm the actual product, applicable requirements and transaction terms with the responsible parties before ordering.
