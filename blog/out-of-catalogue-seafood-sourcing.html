Out-of-Catalogue Seafood Sourcing: From an Incomplete Request to a Comparable Offer

Published 2026-11-08 · Updated 2026-11-13
Published by Yantai Dingdingmiao Seafood

You can start a seafood sourcing request before every specification is settled. Send what you know, identify what must stay unchanged, and mark the unknowns. We can then clarify the requirement and investigate supplier options. A product missing from our catalogue can still be requested; a sourcing request does not establish availability or reserve stock.

An incomplete request, worked through

Illustrative example only: a distributor asks for “frozen white-fish portions, around 2 tonnes, 10 kg cartons, for Jebel Ali.” These quantities and packing are hypothetical buyer preferences, not our minimum order, inventory or shipment offer.

The useful next step is a clarification sheet, not an invented price. The buyer can divide each line into fixed, preferred or unknown:

Buyer saysWhat still needs a decisionHow to record it
White-fish portionsSpecies, skin/bone specification, processing and intended useSpecies not decided; identify each proposed species explicitly. Do not treat every white fish as interchangeable.
Around 2 tonnesTrial or repeat order, quantity tolerance and supplier minimumTarget 2,000 kg; supplier to confirm feasibility. No minimum assumed.
10 kg cartonsInner packs, net seafood weight, glaze basis and labelingOuter carton preference 10 kg; weight basis and inner layout still open.
For Jebel AliBuyer/importer requirements, named delivery point, shipping window and quotation scopeDestination requested; route, documents and commercial terms not yet confirmed.

If you only have a photograph, say what it is meant to illustrate: cut, pack or size. A photograph alone does not establish species, certification or current supply. For U.S. naming discussions, the FDA Seafood List distinguishes scientific, common and acceptable market names; it is not a universal import approval list.

Control proposed alternatives

Separate a supplier's alternative from an answer matching your brief. Suppose the example buyer later fixes the species and portion size but can consider two inner-pack layouts. Ask for an offer for each layout against the same fixed specification. Keep any different species or size in a separate proposal until the buyer has accepted that change.

  • Must match: the buyer's confirmed requirements.
  • May vary: clearly listed alternatives that the buyer is willing to review.
  • Still unknown: details awaiting a named party's answer.

A blank field remains unknown. It is not permission to substitute or evidence that two offers match. Where no suitable option is confirmed, keep that status explicit instead of presenting a nearby product as a confirmed match.

Review a partial match before changing the request

A supplier reply can answer part of a brief without matching the whole requirement. Put the difference in writing before deciding whether the option is useful. For the illustrative fish-portion request above, an offer with the right species but a different pack remains a proposed alternative until the buyer accepts that pack.

Supplier replyBuyer decisionKeep in the record
Matches fixed fields; a preferred pack differsCompare the changed pack, weight basis and costs before accepting or declining it.The exact changed field, the buyer's answer and the revised offer reference.
A fixed field differs, or required evidence is absentKeep the option on hold. Ask for a matching proposal or the missing evidence; do not label it approved.What remains unresolved, who must answer and the next review point.
No suitable source is confirmedChoose whether to continue searching, relax an explicitly named condition or stop the line.Not sourced, with the buyer's next instruction. No stock or lead time assumed.

Changing one field can affect others. A different size may change the offered quantity or pack; a revised pack may change carton dimensions and freight assumptions. Ask for the affected terms to be restated in one dated revision. Do not combine a price from the first offer with a different specification from a later message and treat the result as a confirmed quote.

For several products, apply this review to every line and assess the proposed shipment separately. The mixed seafood order checklist shows how to handle separate minimums, ready dates and a line that remains unavailable. Accepting a product alternative does not by itself approve the combined shipment.

What makes the next offer comparable?

Before comparing prices, put the requested specification beside the supplier's offered value. Include the unit billed, net seafood weight and packing, currency, quantity, validity, service scope, proposed timing and all open conditions. Keep each offer's date and revision so an old answer is not mistaken for the final one.

Our downloadable RFQ and supplier-response comparison sheet provides columns for the requirement, offered value, evidence reference, decision, responsible party and next step. It is a blank working tool, not a quotation or supplier approval.

For delivery terms, ICC explains why the chosen rule, named place and version matter. Record the actual agreed product, payment and other transaction terms too.

Agree the sourcing scope

Dingdingmiao is a trading and sourcing company. Depending on the agreed engagement, our role can include organizing requirements, investigating supply options and coordinating quotations. Agree which party supplies the goods, which checks are included, which questions need independent confirmation, and how any service fee or commission is calculated before proceeding.

Global inquiry coverage does not mean every product can be supplied to every destination. Availability, supplier and plant records, destination requirements, samples, inspection arrangements and shipment feasibility need review for the actual request. Read our sourcing service and cooperation process.

Send a useful first message

Tell us your company and contact, product or reference description, intended use, target quantity, destination, timing, fixed requirements and acceptable alternatives. Write “not decided” for missing information. You can use the full RFQ template or start with a short request and add details by email.

Please share only material you are entitled to disclose. Remove unrelated customer data, confidential prices and private contract details from reference documents.
