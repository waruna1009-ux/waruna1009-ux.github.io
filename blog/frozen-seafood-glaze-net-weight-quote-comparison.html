Frozen Seafood Quotes: Compare Net Weight, Glaze and Cost per Carton

Published 2026-10-23 · Updated 2026-10-23
Published by Yantai Dingdingmiao Seafood

Compare the amount paid with the seafood weight excluding glaze, using the same product specification and cost scope. A lower carton price can cost more per kilogram of seafood. First establish what every weight and percentage means; do not subtract glaze from an already deglazed net weight.

Label the three quantities before calculating

QuantityRecord it explicitly
Seafood weight excluding glazeThe seafood itself after the agreed deglazing determination.
Glazed product weightSeafood plus the protective ice coating, without the packaging.
Gross shipping weightThe packed weight; specify whether carton, pallet and other packing are included.

Use those full descriptions on your comparison sheet. The single word “net” in a commercial message is not enough to resolve an ambiguous offer. Ask the supplier to state the measurement and pricing basis in writing.

Worked example: the cheaper carton costs more

Illustrative arithmetic only; these are not current seafood prices or offers. Assume the species, grade, quantity, currency and included services are identical, and the seafood weights below have been established on the same basis.

Offer B has the lower carton price, but its cost per kilogram of seafood is about 6.9% higher. This does not by itself select the better supplier: specification, documents, product suitability and included costs still need comparison.

Comparison formula: carton price ÷ kilograms of seafood excluding glaze. Keep unrounded values while comparing; round only for presentation.

A glaze percentage needs a denominator

“20% glaze” can be interpreted differently in a commercial discussion. Ask for the formula as well as the percentage.

  • If ice is 20% of a 10 kg glazed product weight, the seafood portion is 10 × (1 − 0.20) = 8 kg.
  • If ice weight equals 20% of the seafood weight and the glazed product weighs 10 kg, the seafood portion is 10 ÷ 1.20 = about 8.33 kg.
  • If the quotation already states 10 kg of seafood excluding glaze, do not deduct another 20% from that 10 kg.

These examples explain arithmetic under stated assumptions. They are not a laboratory method or permission to choose a labeling definition. Agree the applicable measurement method and legal label declaration for the destination.

Separate glaze from thaw loss and added ingredients

Ice removed during deglazing, liquid lost during thawing, and water or ingredients incorporated into a product are different questions. A glaze percentage does not establish total moisture, thawing yield or processing performance. Ask for the relevant specification and agreed evaluation method if those measures matter to your application.

The FDA’s September 2025 short-weighting update explains that protective glaze is used on frozen seafood, but its weight must not inflate the declared seafood net quantity. This is a U.S. reference; confirm the current requirements for your destination.

Five questions to put to the supplier

  1. What seafood weight excluding glaze is supplied per inner pack and per carton?
  2. What is the glaze calculation basis, and how will weight be checked?
  3. Is the quoted price per carton, glazed kilogram or kilogram of seafood excluding glaze?
  4. Which packing, freight, inspection and service costs are included?
  5. How will the approved specification, label, packing list and invoice remain consistent?

If the answers are incomplete, leave that offer unranked on price until the uncertainty is resolved. Send the comparison fields to Dingdingmiao when you want help organizing a sourcing requirement or coordinating clarified quotations.

Buyer questions

Is a high glaze percentage automatically evidence of poor quality?

No. Glaze alone does not determine product quality. Check the product specification, agreed ice coating, seafood weight, measurement method and suitability for your use.

Can I compare two offers using only their price per kilogram?

Only when the kilogram refers to the same weight basis and the product specification and included costs also match. Otherwise normalize the offers or ask for clarification first.
