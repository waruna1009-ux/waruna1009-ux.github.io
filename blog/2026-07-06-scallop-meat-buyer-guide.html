Scallop Meat Sizes: Count Units, Net Weight and Packing Questions

Published 2026-07-06 · Updated 2026-10-23
Published by Yantai Dingdingmiao Seafood

A scallop size such as U15 or 20/30 is incomplete without its count unit and measurement basis. Ask whether the grade means pieces per pound or per kilogram, how pieces are counted, and whether the weight is measured after deglazing. Keep these definitions attached to the quote.

Confirm what the size label means

Buyers may receive a count range, an under-count designation or a supplier’s internal grade. Do not translate a bare number into a fixed physical size. Have the supplier define the label, the unit, the condition of the product when measured and the acceptable variation within the lot.

For a purely mathematical illustration, 20–30 pieces per pound corresponds to about 44–66 pieces per kilogram, because one kilogram is about 2.205 pounds. That does not make it a 20–30 pieces-per-kilogram product. Actual grading and acceptance require the agreed sampling and count method.

Record species, product form and processing

Specify the scientific name if known, the required meat or other product form, whether roe is included, and any trimming requirement. Record declared ingredients and treatments separately from the surface glaze. A commercial species name does not establish catch origin or processing country.

If you need a particular origin or treatment specification, mark it as mandatory. Request supporting records for the proposed lot; an alternative should be offered separately for your approval.

Agree the order review and next step

Ask which documents identify the species, origin, processing facility and shipment, and have the importer confirm current destination requirements. Certificates apply within their scope, not to every product a trading company discusses.

Send Dingdingmiao the count grade with its unit, product form, treatment requirements, packing, quantity and destination. We can clarify the brief and investigate sourcing options. The website is not a stock list or a promise of a particular plant’s approvals.

Buyer questions

Can I send only U15 when asking for scallop meat?

Add the count unit and the supplier’s definition of U15, plus the agreed measurement and tolerance. The grade label alone is not enough to compare quotations.

Are glaze percentage and moisture content the same measure?

No. Surface ice glaze and moisture within the product are different measures. Specify and evaluate each separately if both matter to your order.
