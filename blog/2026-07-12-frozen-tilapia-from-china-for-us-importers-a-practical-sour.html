Frozen Tilapia Buying Checklist: Fillet Size, Packing and Order Review

Published 2026-07-12 · Updated 2026-10-23
Published by Yantai Dingdingmiao Seafood

Describe the tilapia product you want to receive: whole fish or fillet, skin and bone specification, size unit, packing and seafood weight excluding glaze. Ask for the proposed specification and packing layout before comparing offers. There is no single size grade or packing format that fits every buyer.

  2. Put the unit beside the size

Describe the cut without relying on a grade name

For fillets, state skin-on or skinless, bone requirements, trim and any appearance criteria your customer has specified. For whole fish, describe whether it is whole, gutted or headed and gutted. Ask the supplier to explain its abbreviation rather than assuming it means the same preparation as yours.

If your downstream customer has a reference drawing or specification, use its version number. Product photographs can clarify the discussion but do not verify species, safety, grade or the contents of a future shipment.

Put the unit beside the size

RequirementWhat to clarify
Fillet or portion sizeWeight per piece, stated in g or oz, with the acceptable range and tolerance.
Whole fish sizeIndividual fish weight or an explicitly defined count basis.
Net contentsSeafood weight excluding glaze per pack and carton.
PackingNumber of packs, arrangement, label language and carton marks.

A label such as “premium” or “standard” is incomplete without an agreed description. Do not assign a universal thawing-yield threshold or infer quality from fillet color alone. Establish the criteria and evaluation method appropriate to the product and buyer.

Ask for a packing layout

Specify whether you need individually frozen pieces, separate inner bags, interleaving or an agreed block format. Request a photo or diagram showing the number and orientation of pieces or packs. Loose pieces, bags and blocks are different requirements; a vague style name does not describe them adequately.

Confirm the packing configuration can be produced for the proposed quantity. Sample packaging may differ from final production, so record any difference before approving an order.

Review the destination and shipment separately

For U.S. product naming, consult the current FDA Seafood List with the importer. This naming reference is not an approval of a supplier or shipment. Ask the importer and relevant advisers to confirm the product, facility, label and import checks that apply.

Request the actual loading plan, route and proposed dates from the parties handling the shipment. A generic container tonnage or transit-time table cannot confirm a particular booking. Smaller trial shipments depend on cold-chain service availability and costs.

Send a tilapia requirement that can be reviewed

Include the product form, size basis, required trim, weight and packing, quantity, destination, shipment window and documents to check. Mark alternatives separately. Dingdingmiao can investigate the requirement and coordinate quotations, including products not shown in our catalog; stock and order feasibility are confirmed case by case.

Buyer questions

Is there one standard tilapia fillet size for all buyers?

No. State the piece-weight range, unit and tolerance required by your customer, then confirm what the proposed supplier can offer.

Does a packing description prove the product is suitable for import?

No. Packing is one specification item. Product identity, the proposed facility, labels, records and destination requirements must also be reviewed.
