Quotations & sourcing

Frozen Mackerel Quotations: Align Product, Weight and Delivery Basis

Published 2026-07-06 · Updated 2026-10-23
Published by Yantai Dingdingmiao Seafood

Before ranking mackerel offers by price, check that the product, quantity, weight basis and delivery scope match. Put specification differences in one column and commercial differences in another. A low headline price does not explain what is supplied or which costs remain for the buyer.

In this guide
  1. Start with one approved specification
  2. Keep a comparison sheet
  3. Delivery letters need a named location
  4. Use current offers, not a generic price table

Start with one approved specification

Use the same species, preparation, size unit, tolerance, freezing description, seafood weight and packing in each request. A quotation that changes one of those fields should identify the change explicitly. Decide whether the alternative is workable before treating it as a cheaper equivalent.

Ask how the proposed quantity fits the packing configuration. Container capacity depends on the actual shipment and loading constraints; a generic tonnage is not a confirmed loading plan.

Keep a comparison sheet

ColumnWhat to enter
Product matchMatched fields, alternatives and unresolved specification questions.
Price and validityCurrency, unit, quoted quantity, validity date and conditions.
Weight basisSeafood kg excluding glaze per pack and carton, plus the pricing unit.
Delivery scopeAgreed term and named point, included costs and excluded services.
ReadinessProposed dates, documents to check and responsible parties.

Do not fill missing entries with assumptions. Where information is incomplete, ask a focused clarification question and leave the comparison open.

Delivery letters need a named location

ICC’s Incoterms guidance explains why the named place and rule version matter. These rules address delivery responsibilities, costs and risk; they do not replace the product specification or payment agreement. Have the parties agree the appropriate rule for the transport arrangement.

Ask for freight, inspection, packing and sourcing-service items to be identified consistently. A quote covering a different delivery scope cannot be compared fairly just by looking at its per-kilogram number.

Use current offers, not a generic price table

Request an offer for the actual requirement and date. Species, size, product form, packing, quantity, source and included services can differ between offers. This guide does not publish a market price or suggest that a previously discussed offer remains valid.

Dingdingmiao can help structure a mackerel sourcing inquiry and coordinate clarification of potential offers. Send the desired specification, quantity, destination and timing, and identify where alternatives can be considered.

Buyer questions

Can I compare an offer to an origin port with one delivered farther inland?

Only after reconciling the delivery scope and relevant costs. Ask the parties to identify their responsibilities and price components clearly.

Is the lowest price per kilogram necessarily the lowest product cost?

No. Confirm the weight basis, product match and included costs first. An offer based on a different seafood weight or specification is not directly equivalent.
