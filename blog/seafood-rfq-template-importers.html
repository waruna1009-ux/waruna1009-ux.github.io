Quotations & sourcing

Seafood RFQ Template: Turn a Buying Requirement into Comparable Offers

Published 2026-10-23 · Updated 2026-10-23
Published by Yantai Dingdingmiao Seafood

Start with the seafood you need, what must stay fixed and where you can accept alternatives. Send the same brief to each supplier, and ask them to identify every deviation. This makes the first replies easier to compare, including when you need a product outside our catalog.

In this guide
  1. Start with the buying decision
  2. A copyable buying brief
  3. Ask for an answer against each requirement

Start with the buying decision

A long product description is not always a useful purchase brief. A wholesaler may need a particular carton format; a processor may care more about the cut and usable output; a distributor may need a label approved for its market. State your intended use so the supplier knows which details matter.

Divide your request into required, preferred and open to alternatives. For example, the destination and species may be fixed while packing can be discussed. A supplier should not silently replace a required detail to reach a target price.

A copyable buying brief

Copy this into an email and complete what you know. Write “to be confirmed” for missing details rather than guessing.

Company and contact:
Destination country and port or delivery place:
Product name and scientific name, if known:
Intended use and sales channel:
Required product form / cut / processing:
Size or count, unit and acceptable tolerance:
Required net seafood weight, excluding glaze:
Glaze specification and how it is calculated:
Inner packs, carton configuration and label needs:
Target order quantity and quantity unit:
Requested shipment window:
Requested delivery term, named place and version:
Documents or customer requirements to check:
Must remain unchanged:
Alternatives I can consider:
Please list any difference from this brief separately.

A photo or previous specification can help when the name is unclear. It is a starting reference, not confirmation of species, grade or availability. Do not send unrelated customer records or confidential supplier contracts.

Ask for an answer against each requirement

Response fieldWhat a useful reply should show
SpecificationExact match, proposed alternative or still unconfirmed; identify the relevant line.
Commercial basisCurrency, price unit, quantity basis, validity and payment proposal.
Weight and packingSeafood weight excluding glaze, packaging configuration and the basis used for pricing.
Timing and documentsWhat has been checked, what is conditional and who must confirm the remaining points.
ServicesWhich inspection, labeling, shipment or sourcing services are included and what costs extra.

If one offer leaves a field blank, keep it visibly unconfirmed. A blank does not mean it matches your brief. Compare technical differences before negotiating the headline price.

Keep naming and delivery terms precise

For U.S. labeling discussions, the FDA Seafood List helps distinguish scientific, common and acceptable market names. A supplier nickname alone may not identify the product clearly.

For delivery terms, ICC recommends specifying the chosen rule, named port or place, and version. Also request the actual payment, product and timing terms; three delivery letters do not define the whole transaction.

Use the brief when the product is not in our catalog

Send your requirement to Dingdingmiao even if you cannot find a matching product page. We review the request, clarify gaps, investigate supply options and coordinate quotations where a workable match can be found. A sourcing inquiry does not reserve stock or confirm an order.

Agree our service scope and any fee or commission before cooperation. Keep the approved product specification attached to the final quotation so later substitutions can be identified.

Buyer questions

Can I request a quotation before I know every specification?

Yes. Send what is known and mark the rest as undecided. We can help organize the questions and possible alternatives before seeking comparable quotations.

Do you source seafood outside the website catalog?

Yes. Dingdingmiao welcomes unlisted seafood requests. Supply, documents, quantity and shipment feasibility must be checked for the individual request.
