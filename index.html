Questions from seafood buyers

What does Yantai Dingdingmiao Seafood do?

We are a seafood trading and sourcing company in Yantai, China. We welcome inquiries across seafood categories, investigate supply options, coordinate quotations and support agreed transactions for global buyers.

Which seafood products can I inquire about?

You can inquire about any seafood requirement, including products not shown in our catalog. Fish, shrimp, squid, shellfish and processed seafood are examples. We review availability, packing, origin and shipment feasibility for each inquiry.

How do I request a seafood quotation?

Email waruna825@outlook.com or contact WhatsApp +86 13954508112 with product/species, size or form, packing and net weight, quantity, destination port and shipment window.

Can you confirm certificates and minimum order quantities?

We check plant and product documents, destination requirements, order quantity and loading plans case by case before confirming an offer. The website is a sourcing catalog, not a guarantee of stock or certification.
