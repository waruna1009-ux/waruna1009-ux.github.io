Specification checklist for your quotation

SpeciesAtlantic salmon (Salmo salar) or a specified Pacific salmon species. Confirm the scientific name in the offer.
Product formsWhole round, headed and gutted, fillets and steaks are catalog references; discuss portion requirements with us.
Whole-fish reference grades1–3kg, 3–5kg and 5kg+. Fillet and portion orders require their own weight and trim specification.
Processing detailsState skin-on or skinless, pin-bone requirements, trim level and portion tolerance.
Freezing and packingDiscuss IQF or block frozen options, bag and carton weights, labels and destination documentation.

These are catalog reference options, not a live stock list. Tell us which fields are essential and which may be discussed.

Separate species, origin and processing requirements

A salmon order should identify the species and raw-material origin as well as the processing location. For fillets and portions, agree on skin, pin bones, trim and weight tolerance in writing. If the intended use requires particular handling or documentation, state it before requesting an offer. A product name alone does not confirm suitability for every use.

Questions from seafood buyers

Can I inquire about frozen salmon fillets or portions?

Yes. Send the species, skin and pin-bone requirements, trim level, weight range, packing and quantity. The specific supply option is checked before quotation.

Does sourcing from China mean the salmon was farmed in China?

No. Supplier location, raw-material origin and processing location are separate fields. We ask buyers to confirm the required origin and review the documents for each offer.

Can you confirm certifications before an order?

Certification and plant-document availability must be checked for the selected product, partner plant and destination. We do not treat a catalog listing as proof of a particular certification.

Send a complete inquiry

Include your company, product and species, size or form, net weight and packing, estimated quantity, destination port and shipment window. Add any required plant documents or label requirements.
