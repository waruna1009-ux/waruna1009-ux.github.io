Specification checklist for your quotation

SpeciesIllex argentinus and Todarodes pacificus are catalog references. Specify your required species or ask us to check suitable options.
FormsWhole round, tubes, rings or strips; specify cleaning, skin and wing requirements.
SizingWhole squid catalog grades: 100–200g, 200–300g, 300–400g and 400g+. For tubes or rings, provide dimensions instead of a whole-fish weight.
FreezingIQF or block frozen, subject to the selected product and packing.
Packing and weightProvide bag and carton weights, net weight and glaze requirements. Confirm any treatment and label declarations before order approval.

These are catalog reference options, not a live stock list. Tell us which fields are essential and which may be discussed.

Write a squid specification that a supplier can quote

For tubes, include length, width and whether skin, wings or membrane must be removed. For rings, specify ring width and the source material. For whole squid, use weight grades and cleaning requirements. Keep species, treatment declarations and net weight visible in the written offer, so a lower price does not hide a different specification.

Questions from seafood buyers

Do you handle frozen squid tubes and rings?

Tubes and rings are listed in our catalog, alongside whole round squid and strips. Availability, dimensions, processing and packing are confirmed after reviewing the buyer specification.

What should a squid importer send for a quotation?

Send species, product form, size or dimensions, net weight and glaze requirements, packing, quantity, destination port and expected shipment window.

Are all squid products from the same origin?

Do not infer catch origin from the supplier location. Ask for the raw-material origin, processing location and applicable product documents for the specific offer.

Send a complete inquiry

Include your company, product and species, size or form, net weight and packing, estimated quantity, destination port and shipment window. Add any required plant documents or label requirements.
