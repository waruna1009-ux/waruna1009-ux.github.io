Avoid confusion between length and count grades

Sardine offers may use length, individual weight or pieces per kilogram. Put the measurement and grading tolerance in your request so suppliers compare the same specification. Before agreeing on a carton price, confirm net fish weight, freezing method and packing condition. Destination-specific document and label requirements should be settled before shipment planning.

Questions from seafood buyers

Which frozen sardine grades can I ask about?

Our catalog lists 10–12cm, 12–15cm and 15cm+ reference grades. Current availability and grading tolerance are confirmed for the specific inquiry.

Can you help with a sardine inquiry for an African market?

Send the destination country and port with your product and packing specification. We review supply options and document requirements for the intended destination before confirming an offer.

What determines the quoted carton weight?

The written quotation should distinguish net fish weight from glaze and packaging. Confirm the agreed weight basis and carton marking with the supplier before the order.
