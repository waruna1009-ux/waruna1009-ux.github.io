From a buying request to a workable offer

  1. Define the requirement. Product and species, form, size, net weight, packing, quantity, destination and timing.
  2. Investigate supply options. Review available suppliers and product fit. Request relevant specifications and documents.
  3. Coordinate a comparable quotation. Clarify price basis, payment, validity, loading plan and responsibilities. Any sourcing fee or commission arrangement is agreed before cooperation.
  4. Review the proposed transaction. Confirm supplier identity, product documents, labels and inspection requirements before order approval.
  5. Coordinate agreed follow-through. Assist with buyer–supplier communication, shipment preparation and document checks within the agreed scope.

Supply, certification, delivery schedules and destination eligibility are confirmed case by case. We do not promise availability before reviewing the request.

What to include in your inquiry

Company and contact name; product/species; photo or specification sheet if available; quantity; target form and size; packing/net weight; destination country and port; expected shipment window; and required documents. A target price or previous specification can help compare alternatives, if you have one.

Working with Dingdingmiao

Are you a seafood factory or a trading company?

Yantai Dingdingmiao is a seafood trading and sourcing company in Yantai, China. We coordinate with suppliers and partner processors; our website does not represent an owned factory or a warehouse stock list.

Can you find a product that is not in your catalog?

Yes. Send the product name, species if known, specification or photo, quantity, packing and destination. We investigate supply options and coordinate quotations. Availability and suitability must be confirmed before an order.

Which seafood categories can I ask about?

We welcome inquiries across seafood categories: fish, shrimp and other crustaceans, squid and other cephalopods, shellfish and processed seafood. State whether you need frozen, chilled, live, dried or another form so feasibility can be reviewed.

How does a sourcing or commission arrangement work?

We clarify the requested service, supplier and quotation responsibilities, and any service fee or commission basis before cooperation. Product price, payment terms, inspection scope and shipment responsibilities are agreed for the specific transaction.

Can buyers outside China work with you?

We welcome inquiries from global importers, wholesalers, distributors and processors. Supply origin, plant eligibility, labels and import documents are checked for the destination; a global inquiry service does not guarantee every product can enter every market.
