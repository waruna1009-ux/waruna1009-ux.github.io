Which buying task do you need help with?

  • Find an unlisted product. Share a product name, description or reference photo. We can organize the missing questions and investigate possible sources against your requirements.

Agree who confirms each part

Before an order, put the parties and their responsibilities beside the proposed product and quotation. The following is a discussion checklist; the actual scope is agreed for your request.

PartyPoints to confirm together
BuyerIntended use and destination, fixed requirements, acceptable alternatives and the person who approves specification or artwork changes.
Supplier or processorThe offered product and pack, quantity, current availability, supporting records and conditions attached to price or timing.
DingdingmiaoThe supplier search, clarification and quotation coordination requested; any agreed document or shipment support; and the service fee or commission basis.

Also identify who sells and invoices the goods, who receives payment, and who handles any inspection, freight or destination review. An introduction, supplier reply or sourcing fee does not settle these responsibilities by itself.

Keep each supplier's offered values separate from your requirements. Where a source, document or condition remains unconfirmed, record the open point and the next person to answer. A quotation is ready for your review only when its scope and remaining conditions are clear.

Need your own label or a different pack?

Include the packing request with the product brief so the supplier can assess it. Buyer-branded packing is subject to supplier confirmation; a logo or sample image alone is not an approved production specification.

  • Pack layout: inner-pack size, packs per carton, seafood weight basis and target quantity.
  • Artwork: the file name and revision, target market and language, and who will approve the product information and final artwork.
  • Quotation scope: ask whether samples, printing setup, packaging materials and changes are included, and which minimums or timing conditions apply.
  • Approval record: identify the exact sample or proof being reviewed and any unresolved points. Agree the applicable product and destination checks before confirming production.

For several products, use the mixed seafood order checklist to review each line's minimum, packing and ready date before assessing the shared shipment.

Working with Dingdingmiao

Are you a seafood factory or a trading company?

Yantai Dingdingmiao is a seafood trading and sourcing company in Yantai, China. We coordinate with suppliers and partner processors; our website does not represent an owned factory or a warehouse stock list.

Can you find a product that is not in your catalog?

Yes. Send the product name, species if known, specification or photo, quantity, packing and destination. We investigate supply options and coordinate quotations. Availability and suitability must be confirmed before an order.

Which seafood categories can I ask about?

We welcome inquiries across seafood categories: fish, shrimp and other crustaceans, squid and other cephalopods, shellfish and processed seafood. State whether you need frozen, chilled, live, dried or another form so feasibility can be reviewed.

How does a sourcing or commission arrangement work?

We clarify the requested service, supplier and quotation responsibilities, and any service fee or commission basis before cooperation. Product price, payment terms, inspection scope and shipment responsibilities are agreed for the specific transaction.

Can buyers outside China work with you?

We welcome inquiries from global importers, wholesalers, distributors and processors. Supply origin, plant eligibility, labels and import documents are checked for the destination; a global inquiry service does not guarantee every product can enter every market.

Can I ask about buyer-branded packing?

Yes. Send the product specification, pack layout, quantity, target market and artwork revision if available. We can ask suppliers to assess the request. Packaging availability, artwork approval, relevant checks, costs and timing must be confirmed for the proposed order.
