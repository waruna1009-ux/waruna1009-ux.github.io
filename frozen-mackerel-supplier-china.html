Questions from seafood buyers

Can I request 300–500g frozen mackerel?

Yes. 300–500g is a reference grade in our catalog. Send the quantity, packing and destination port so current availability and an order-specific quotation can be checked.

Is the quotation for sea-frozen or land-frozen mackerel?

State the freezing method you require in the inquiry. The offer must identify the actual processing and freezing method; they should not be assumed from a product photograph.

Can you quote for West Africa, the Middle East or Southeast Asia?

Send the importing country and destination port. We review the product specification, packing, shipment plan and required documents for that destination before confirming an offer.

Send a complete inquiry

Include your company, product and species, size or form, net weight and packing, estimated quantity, destination port and shipment window. Add any required plant documents or label requirements.
